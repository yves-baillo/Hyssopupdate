ABOUT HYSSOP

A ministry built through music.

Hyssop Choir is a gospel choir from Rwanda whose ministry is centered on worship, gospel music and sharing the message of Jesus Christ.

The choir began in 2004 as a student choir and continued to grow in its music, ministry and gospel work.

"Faith • Growth • Worship • Ministry"
